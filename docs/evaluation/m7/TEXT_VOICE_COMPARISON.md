# Text / Voice Same-Text Comparison

The locked experiment passed identical question, answer text, profile, and expected points through the unchanged production Text and Voice seams without STT.

- Mean absolute score difference: 0.9594
- Median: 1.0
- P95: 2.5
- Exact tier agreement: 81.25%
- Adjacent tier agreement: 100%
- Score Spearman: 0.9448
- Material disagreement rate (absolute delta >= 2): 12.5% (4/32)

Cross-mode correlation is high, but the P95 and material-disagreement tail remain operational risks.
