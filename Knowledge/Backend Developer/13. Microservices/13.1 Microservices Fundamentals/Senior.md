- Define invariants, governance rules, compatibility policies, and risk controls related to **Independent Deployment**.

- Set standards for observability, resilience, security review, capacity planning, and lifecycle management of **Independent Deployment**.
- Identify when **Independent Deployment** should be standardized, constrained, replaced, or intentionally avoided.
