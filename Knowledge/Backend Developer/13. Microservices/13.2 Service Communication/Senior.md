# Senior

- Establish organization-level principles and architectural guidance for infrastructure that routes published events to interested consumers.
- Make system-wide decisions about subscriptions, filtering, delivery guarantees, ordering, and coupling under reliability, security, scalability, compliance, and cost constraints.
- Define invariants, governance rules, compatibility policies, and risk controls related to **Event Bus**.
- Evaluate cross-service failure scenarios, systemic coupling, migration paths, and long-term operational consequences involving **Event Bus**.
- Set standards for observability, resilience, security review, capacity planning, and lifecycle management of **Event Bus**.
- Identify when **Event Bus** should be standardized, constrained, replaced, or intentionally avoided.
- Communicate strategic trade-offs and decision rationale to engineering, product, security, and operations stakeholders.
