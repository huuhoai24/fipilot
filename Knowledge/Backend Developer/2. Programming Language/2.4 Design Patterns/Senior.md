- Make system-wide decisions about constructor, setter, and interface injection trade-offs under reliability, security, scalability, compliance, and cost constraints.
- Define invariants, governance rules, compatibility policies, and risk controls related to **Dependency Injection**.
- Evaluate cross-service failure scenarios, systemic coupling, migration paths, and long-term operational consequences involving **Dependency Injection**.
- Set standards for observability, resilience, security review, capacity planning, and lifecycle management of **Dependency Injection**.
- Identify when **Dependency Injection** should be standardized, constrained, replaced, or intentionally avoided.
- Communicate strategic trade-offs and decision rationale to engineering, product, security, and operations stakeholders.
