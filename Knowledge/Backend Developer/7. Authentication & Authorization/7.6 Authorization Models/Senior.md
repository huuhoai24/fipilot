# Senior

- Establish organization-level principles and architectural guidance for authorization decisions produced by centrally expressed policies.
- Make system-wide decisions about policy decision and enforcement points, conflict resolution, versioning, and explainability under reliability, security, scalability, compliance, and cost constraints.
- Define invariants, governance rules, compatibility policies, and risk controls related to **Policy-Based Access Control (PBAC)**.
- Evaluate cross-service failure scenarios, systemic coupling, migration paths, and long-term operational consequences involving **Policy-Based Access Control (PBAC)**.
- Set standards for observability, resilience, security review, capacity planning, and lifecycle management of **Policy-Based Access Control (PBAC)**.
- Identify when **Policy-Based Access Control (PBAC)** should be standardized, constrained, replaced, or intentionally avoided.
- Communicate strategic trade-offs and decision rationale to engineering, product, security, and operations stakeholders.
