# Senior

- Establish organization-level principles and architectural guidance for requests whose execution depends on validators or resource state.
- Make system-wide decisions about If-None-Match, If-Modified-Since, If-Match, caching, and lost-update prevention under reliability, security, scalability, compliance, and cost constraints.
- Define invariants, governance rules, compatibility policies, and risk controls related to **Conditional Requests**.
- Evaluate cross-service failure scenarios, systemic coupling, migration paths, and long-term operational consequences involving **Conditional Requests**.
- Set standards for observability, resilience, security review, capacity planning, and lifecycle management of **Conditional Requests**.
- Identify when **Conditional Requests** should be standardized, constrained, replaced, or intentionally avoided.
- Communicate strategic trade-offs and decision rationale to engineering, product, security, and operations stakeholders.
