# Senior

- Establish organization-level principles and architectural guidance for concentric architecture with domain at the center and dependencies pointing inward.
- Make system-wide decisions about domain services, application services, infrastructure, and dependency rules under reliability, security, scalability, compliance, and cost constraints.
- Define invariants, governance rules, compatibility policies, and risk controls related to **Onion Architecture**.
- Evaluate cross-service failure scenarios, systemic coupling, migration paths, and long-term operational consequences involving **Onion Architecture**.
- Set standards for observability, resilience, security review, capacity planning, and lifecycle management of **Onion Architecture**.
- Identify when **Onion Architecture** should be standardized, constrained, replaced, or intentionally avoided.
- Communicate strategic trade-offs and decision rationale to engineering, product, security, and operations stakeholders.
