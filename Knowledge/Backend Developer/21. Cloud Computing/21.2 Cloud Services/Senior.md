# Senior

- Establish organization-level principles and architectural guidance for flat, key-addressed storage of immutable or replaceable objects with metadata.
- Make system-wide decisions about buckets, consistency, durability, lifecycle, multipart upload, and access control under reliability, security, scalability, compliance, and cost constraints.
- Define invariants, governance rules, compatibility policies, and risk controls related to **Object Storage**.
- Evaluate cross-service failure scenarios, systemic coupling, migration paths, and long-term operational consequences involving **Object Storage**.
- Set standards for observability, resilience, security review, capacity planning, and lifecycle management of **Object Storage**.
- Identify when **Object Storage** should be standardized, constrained, replaced, or intentionally avoided.
- Communicate strategic trade-offs and decision rationale to engineering, product, security, and operations stakeholders.
