# Junior

- Explain how PII (Personally Identifiable Information) supports discoverability, accountability, protection, and regulatory compliance.
- Analyze ownership, classification, access, retention, lineage, and metadata requirements.
- Understand least privilege, encryption, masking, and audit concepts where relevant.
- Recognize governance gaps and conflicting definitions.
- Evaluate whether policies and controls for PII (Personally Identifiable Information) are clear and enforceable.
