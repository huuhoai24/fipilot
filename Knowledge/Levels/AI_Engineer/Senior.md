# AI Engineer - Senior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the nature, limitations and long-term implications of AI methods.
- Evaluate solutions at the overall AI system level.
- Translate business objectives into technical decisions.
- Build decision frameworks rather than simply selecting an algorithm.
- Evaluate trade-offs among accuracy, latency, throughput, memory, compute, cost, reliability and maintainability.
- Understand the relationships among data, models, infrastructure and business objectives.
- Assess data risk, model risk and operational risk.
- Evaluate foundation models, fine-tuning, RAG and hybrid AI systems based on applicable conditions.
- Understand governance, privacy, fairness, explainability and compliance at a system level.
- Determine when AI should not be used or when a specific method should not be applied.
- Challenge the premise of the problem before making decisions.

### Evaluation Focus

- Nature.
- Limitations.
- Decision framework.
- Architecture.
- Multi-dimensional trade-offs.
- Risk.
- Governance.
- Long-term implications.
- Systems thinking.
