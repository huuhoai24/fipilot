# Data Scientist - Senior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the nature and limitations of data analysis and Machine Learning methods.
- Translate business objectives into analytical strategy and decision frameworks.
- Evaluate trade-offs among prediction quality, interpretability, robustness, cost and business value.
- Understand the relationships among data, models, statistics and business decisions.
- Assess data risk, model risk and decision risk.
- Analyze experimental strategy, causal reasoning and uncertainty in the decision-making process.
- Evaluate foundation models, classical ML and hybrid approaches based on applicable conditions.
- Understand fairness, explainability, privacy and governance in Data Science.
- Assess the long-term impact of models on business outcomes.
- Determine when Machine Learning should not be used or when a model should not be built.
- Challenge the premise of the problem and require clarification of assumptions before drawing conclusions.

### Evaluation Focus

- Nature.
- Limitations.
- Decision framework.
- Statistical reasoning.
- Multi-dimensional trade-offs.
- Business value.
- Governance.
- Risk.
- Long-term implications.
- Systems thinking.
- Ability to challenge assumptions.
