# Business Analyst - Senior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the nature and limitations of Business Analysis at an organizational level.
- Translate business strategy into capability, initiative, portfolio objective and transformation roadmap.
- Analyze the relationships among strategy, operating model, process, data, technology and organization.
- Build decision frameworks rather than simply providing a single solution recommendation.
- Evaluate business problems in the context of multiple units, multiple stakeholders and multiple systems.
- Challenge the premise of a requirement when a solution is proposed before the problem is confirmed.
- Analyze enterprise capability, capability gaps, value streams and strategic alignment.
- Evaluate portfolio trade-offs among value, cost, risk, dependency, urgency and organizational capacity.
- Understand enterprise requirement architecture and the alignment among strategic, business, stakeholder, solution and transition requirements.
- Evaluate governance models for requirements, decisions, changes and traceability.
- Analyze the long-term impact of a solution on operating models, policies, processes, data ownership and organizational structure.
- Understand enterprise data governance, master data, data ownership, data lineage and regulatory obligations.
- Evaluate cross-domain processes, shared capabilities and dependencies among multiple business units.
- Analyze build-versus-buy, standardization-versus-customization and centralization-versus-decentralization.
- Evaluate trade-offs among customer value, operational efficiency, compliance, resilience, cost and strategic flexibility.
- Understand risk appetite, control frameworks, auditability and regulatory constraints.
- Analyze business continuity, operational resilience and critical process dependencies.
- Evaluate transformation roadmaps, transition states and sequencing dependencies.
- Understand organizational change, adoption, incentive alignment and stakeholder resistance at a system level.
- Determine when processes should not be changed, when new systems should not be deployed or when automation should not be applied.
- Evaluate the long-term maintainability of policies, processes, data and the system landscape.
- Establish standards for requirement quality, decision transparency, traceability and benefit realization.
- Evaluate outcomes after deployment rather than only assessing delivery output.
- Analyze the causes when a solution meets requirements but fails to achieve business outcomes.
- Provide decision rationale that can be verified, challenged and reused.
- Balance local needs with the organization's overall objectives and architecture.

### Evaluation Focus

- Strategy alignment.
- Enterprise capability.
- Decision framework.
- Governance.
- Portfolio trade-off.
- Business architecture.
- Data governance.
- Organizational change.
- Operational resilience.
- Long-term implications.
- Benefit realization.
- Systems thinking.
- Ability to challenge assumptions.
