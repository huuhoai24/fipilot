# Tester QA QC - Senior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should understand:

- The principles and limitations of Quality Engineering at organizational scale.
- Converting business objectives into quality strategy.
- Decision frameworks instead of tool-driven decisions.
- Trade-offs among quality, delivery speed, reliability, security, maintainability and cost.
- Enterprise testing architecture.
- Quality governance and engineering standards.
- Organization-wide test automation strategy.
- Reliability engineering, resilience and production quality.
- Quality metrics, SLI, SLO and quality KPIs.
- Risk governance and compliance testing.
- Technical debt and long-term quality implications.
- When testing should be shifted left, shifted right or redesigned.
- Challenging assumptions before defining a quality strategy.

### Evaluation Focus

- Principles.
- Limitations.
- Strategy.
- Governance.
- Decision framework.
- Multi-dimensional trade-offs.
- Reliability.
- Security.
- Cost.
- Long-term implications.
- Systems thinking.
- Ability to challenge assumptions.
