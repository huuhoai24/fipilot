# Full Stack Developer - Intern Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the role of a Full Stack Developer and the relationship among Frontend, Backend and Database.
- Understand the foundational concepts of Web, HTTP, Browser and Server.
- Know the purpose of HTML, CSS, JavaScript and API.
- Describe the request–response flow at a high level.
- Understand CRUD, REST API and Database at a conceptual level.
- Distinguish between client-side and server-side rendering.
- Understand authentication, session and cookie at a basic level.
- Know the purpose of Git, testing and deployment.
- Recognize common errors such as CORS, validation and authentication failure.
- No analysis of architecture or distributed systems is expected.

### Evaluation Focus

- Definitions.
- Purpose.
- Components.
- General workflow.
- Concept differentiation.
- Foundational web knowledge.
