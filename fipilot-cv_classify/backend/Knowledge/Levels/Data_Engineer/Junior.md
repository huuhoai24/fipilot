# Data Engineer - Junior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the operating mechanisms of ETL and ELT.
- Explain the pipeline stages of ingest, transform and serving.
- Understand the relationships among source, storage, processing and analytics.
- Compare data warehouse, data lake and lakehouse.
- Understand the relational model, normalization and denormalization.
- Understand partitioning, indexing and clustering at a mechanistic level.
- Understand batch processing and stream processing.
- Understand file formats such as CSV, JSON, Parquet and ORC.
- Understand schema evolution and schema enforcement.
- Understand data quality, validation and data lineage at a conceptual level.
- Identify bottlenecks and common errors in pipelines.
- Explain basic trade-offs among performance, storage and cost.

### Evaluation Focus

- Mechanisms.
- Causes.
- Basic assumptions.
- Method comparison.
- Basic trade-offs.
- Pipeline and data storage.
