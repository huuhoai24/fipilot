# Data Engineer - Senior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the nature and limitations of data architecture at large scale.
- Translate business objectives into data architecture and data platform strategy.
- Build decision frameworks rather than simply selecting a technology.
- Evaluate trade-offs among scalability, consistency, latency, throughput, reliability, maintainability and cost.
- Understand data mesh, data fabric, lakehouse and modern data platforms based on applicable conditions.
- Evaluate data governance, metadata, lineage, quality and security at an organizational level.
- Analyze distributed systems, fault tolerance and disaster recovery.
- Evaluate storage, compute and workload optimization.
- Understand data contracts, schema evolution and backward compatibility.
- Evaluate platform architecture, multi-tenancy and resource isolation.
- Analyze long-term operational cost and technical debt.
- Determine when a particular architecture or technology should not be used.
- Challenge the premise of the problem before making decisions.

### Evaluation Focus

- Nature.
- Limitations.
- Architecture.
- Decision framework.
- Multi-dimensional trade-offs.
- Governance.
- Reliability.
- Scalability.
- Cost.
- Long-term implications.
- Systems thinking.
- Ability to challenge assumptions.
