# Software Engineer - Senior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the nature and limitations of software architecture at large scale.
- Translate business objectives and quality attributes into architectural decisions.
- Build decision frameworks rather than simply selecting a framework or technology.
- Evaluate trade-offs among performance, scalability, reliability, security, maintainability, delivery speed and cost.
- Understand monolith, modular monolith, microservices, event-driven architecture and service-oriented architecture based on applicable conditions.
- Analyze distributed systems, consistency models, fault tolerance and failure domains.
- Evaluate data ownership, service boundaries, API governance and integration strategy.
- Understand platform engineering, internal developer platforms and engineering enablement at a strategic level.
- Evaluate architectural governance, coding standards, dependency policy and technology lifecycle.
- Understand SLI, SLO, SLA, error budget and reliability engineering.
- Analyze security architecture, trust boundaries, identity, access control and supply chain risk.
- Evaluate build-versus-buy, standardization-versus-flexibility and centralization-versus-autonomy.
- Analyze technical debt, organizational constraints and long-term total cost of ownership.
- Evaluate migration strategy, compatibility, transition architecture and operational risk.
- Determine when microservices, distributed systems or complex architectures should not be used.
- Challenge the premise of the problem before making decisions.
- Assess the long-term impact of technical decisions on the organization, product and ability to evolve the system.

### Evaluation Focus

- Nature.
- Limitations.
- Architecture.
- Decision framework.
- Multi-dimensional trade-offs.
- Distributed systems.
- Reliability.
- Security.
- Governance.
- Cost.
- Long-term implications.
- Systems thinking.
- Ability to challenge assumptions.
