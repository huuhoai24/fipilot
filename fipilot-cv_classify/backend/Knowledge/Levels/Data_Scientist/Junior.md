# Data Scientist - Junior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the operating mechanisms of common Machine Learning algorithms.
- Explain why a particular model works effectively.
- Understand the basic assumptions and conditions for application of each algorithm.
- Understand the relationships among data, feature engineering, model, loss function and evaluation.
- Compare common algorithms by their advantages and disadvantages.
- Understand the bias-variance trade-off, regularization and generalization.
- Understand sampling, cross-validation and hyperparameter tuning at a conceptual level.
- Understand descriptive statistics, data distributions, probability and hypothesis testing at a mechanistic level.
- Identify common model failure cases.
- Explain basic trade-offs among accuracy, interpretability and complexity.

### Evaluation Focus

- Mechanisms.
- Causes.
- Basic assumptions.
- Method comparison.
- Basic trade-offs.
- Statistics and model evaluation.
