# Data Scientist - Middle Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Deeply understand the assumptions and limitations of algorithms.
- Analyze model failure modes and root causes.
- Evaluate trade-offs among accuracy, interpretability, robustness and computational cost.
- Understand feature selection, feature interaction and representation learning.
- Analyze data quality, label quality, sampling bias and class imbalance.
- Understand calibration, uncertainty and confidence estimation.
- Evaluate metrics based on business objectives and data distribution.
- Understand causal inference, hypothesis testing and experimental design at a theoretical level.
- Analyze data drift, concept drift and model degradation.
- Propose methods to validate hypotheses and distinguish symptoms from root causes.
- Assess when a method is no longer appropriate.

### Evaluation Focus

- Analysis.
- Assumptions.
- Failure modes.
- Root causes.
- Trade-offs.
- Statistical reasoning.
- Evaluation.
- Conditions for method selection.
