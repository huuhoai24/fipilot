# Data Scientist - Intern Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the role of a Data Scientist in the data exploration process.
- Understand the foundational concepts of Statistics, Machine Learning and Data Analysis.
- Distinguish among supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning.
- Understand the purpose of features, labels, models, loss functions and evaluation metrics.
- Describe the process from data to predictive model.
- Understand the meaning of common metrics such as Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 and ROC-AUC at a conceptual level.
- Understand overfitting, underfitting and data leakage at a basic level.
- Distinguish among classification, regression, clustering and dimensionality reduction.
- Know the purpose of exploratory data analysis (EDA).
- No deep statistical analysis or advanced model selection is expected.

### Evaluation Focus

- Definitions.
- Purpose.
- Components.
- Data analysis process.
- Concept differentiation.
- Foundational knowledge of ML and Statistics.
