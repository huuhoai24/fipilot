# DevOps Engineer - Middle Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Deeply understand CI/CD architecture and cloud infrastructure.
- Analyze assumptions and limitations of deployment solutions.
- Evaluate trade-offs among availability, scalability, reliability, latency and cost.
- Understand Kubernetes scheduling, networking, storage and resource management.
- Analyze service discovery, ingress, service mesh and traffic routing.
- Understand Infrastructure as Code, immutable infrastructure and GitOps.
- Analyze deployment strategies such as blue-green, rolling, canary and progressive delivery.
- Understand observability including metrics, logs, traces and distributed tracing.
- Analyze failure modes, root causes and recovery strategies.
- Understand auto scaling, capacity planning and resource optimization.
- Evaluate backup, disaster recovery and high availability.
- Understand security architecture, IAM, policy, network segmentation and secret lifecycle.
- Evaluate pipeline reliability, dependency management and artifact integrity.
- Understand when an architecture or technology is no longer appropriate.

### Evaluation Focus

- Analysis.
- Assumptions.
- Failure modes.
- Root causes.
- Trade-offs.
- Reliability.
- Scalability.
- Observability.
- Security.
- Conditions for solution selection.
