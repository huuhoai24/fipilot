# DevOps Engineer - Senior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the nature and limitations of platform engineering and cloud architecture.
- Translate business objectives into DevOps strategy and platform architecture.
- Build decision frameworks rather than simply selecting a technology.
- Evaluate trade-offs among availability, scalability, latency, reliability, security, maintainability and cost.
- Understand multi-cloud, hybrid cloud and multi-region based on applicable conditions.
- Evaluate Kubernetes platforms, service mesh, GitOps and Internal Developer Platforms.
- Understand SLI, SLO, SLA, error budget and reliability engineering.
- Analyze distributed systems, failure domains, fault tolerance and resilience.
- Evaluate disaster recovery, business continuity, RPO and RTO.
- Understand governance, compliance, policy-as-code and supply chain security.
- Evaluate infrastructure lifecycle, platform standardization and operational maturity.
- Analyze technical debt, long-term operational cost and organizational scalability.
- Determine when Kubernetes, microservices or cloud-native architecture should not be used.
- Challenge the premise of the problem before making decisions.

### Evaluation Focus

- Nature.
- Limitations.
- Architecture.
- Decision framework.
- Multi-dimensional trade-offs.
- Reliability.
- Security.
- Governance.
- Cost.
- Long-term implications.
- Systems thinking.
- Ability to challenge assumptions.
