# Business Analyst - Intern Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Correctly understand the role and scope of work of a Business Analyst.
- Understand foundational concepts such as business need, requirement, stakeholder, scope and constraint.
- Distinguish among business requirements, stakeholder requirements, functional requirements and non-functional requirements.
- Understand the purpose of requirements gathering, analysis, validation and management.
- Describe the requirements lifecycle at a high level.
- Understand the role of common documents such as BRD, SRS, user story, use case and acceptance criteria.
- Know common requirements elicitation techniques such as interview, workshop, observation, survey and document analysis.
- Understand the basic components of process flow, use case diagram, activity diagram and data flow.
- Distinguish between current state and future state.
- Understand the concept of stakeholder analysis and the level of stakeholder influence.
- Know the purpose of prioritization, traceability and change management.
- Understand common Agile and Scrum terminology at a basic level.
- Recognize common issues such as ambiguous, inconsistent, incomplete or untestable requirements.
- No deep analysis of business strategy, enterprise architecture or organizational change is expected.

### Evaluation Focus

- Definitions.
- Purpose.
- Components.
- Requirement type differentiation.
- Requirements lifecycle.
- Basic documents and models.
- Common requirement defects.
