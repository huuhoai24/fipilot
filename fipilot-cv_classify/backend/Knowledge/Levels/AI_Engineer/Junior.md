# AI Engineer - Junior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the operating mechanisms of common algorithms and models.
- Explain why a particular method works effectively.
- Understand basic assumptions and conditions for application.
- Understand the relationships among data, models, loss functions, optimizers and evaluation metrics.
- Compare common algorithms or architectures.
- Understand the underlying reasons for the advantages and limitations of each method.
- Understand regularization, optimization and generalization at a mechanistic level.
- Understand embedding, transfer learning and fine-tuning at a conceptual level.
- Identify common failure cases.
- Explain basic trade-offs among accuracy, speed and complexity.

### Evaluation Focus

- Mechanisms.
- Causes.
- Basic assumptions.
- Method comparison.
- Basic trade-offs.
- Failure cases.
