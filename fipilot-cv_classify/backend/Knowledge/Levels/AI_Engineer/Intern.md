# AI Engineer - Intern Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Correctly understand the foundational concepts of AI, Machine Learning and Deep Learning.
- Know the purpose of common algorithms, models and techniques.
- Describe the general flow from data to prediction results.
- Understand the roles of data, features, models, loss functions and evaluation metrics.
- Distinguish among common categories of AI problems.
- Understand the meaning of common terminology.
- Know the basic advantages and limitations of various methods.
- Understand inference, training and fine-tuning at a high level.
- Recognize common errors such as overfitting, underfitting and data leakage.
- No analysis of assumptions or trade-offs is expected.

### Evaluation Focus

- Definitions.
- Purpose.
- Components.
- General workflow.
- Concept differentiation.
