# Back End Developer - Senior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the nature, limitations and long-term implications of backend architecture decisions.
- Evaluate the entire system spanning API, compute, database, cache, messaging and infrastructure.
- Translate business objectives and non-functional requirements into architecture principles.
- Build decision frameworks rather than simply selecting a technology.
- Analyze trade-offs among consistency, availability, latency, throughput, durability, cost and maintainability.
- Evaluate when a modular monolith is better than microservices and vice versa.
- Understand service boundaries, ownership, data ownership and domain decomposition.
- Analyze coupling between services, shared databases, synchronous chains and distributed monoliths.
- Evaluate distributed consistency, saga, outbox, event sourcing and CQRS based on applicable conditions.
- Understand failure domains, blast radius, cascading failures and fault isolation.
- Design reliability strategies based on SLI, SLO, SLA and error budget.
- Evaluate multi-region, active-active, active-passive and disaster recovery trade-offs.
- Understand RPO, RTO, backup, restore, failover and data reconciliation.
- Analyze capacity planning, autoscaling, queue growth and backpressure.
- Evaluate system bottlenecks through end-to-end reasoning rather than local optimization.
- Understand data partitioning, sharding strategy, hot partitions and resharding implications.
- Evaluate database technology based on workload, consistency model, access pattern and operational maturity.
- Analyze schema evolution, data contracts and backward compatibility across multiple consumers.
- Evaluate API governance, versioning strategy and compatibility policy at organizational scale.
- Understand security architecture, zero trust, least privilege, auditability and compliance constraints.
- Analyze threat models, data classification, encryption, key management and incident response.
- Evaluate build-versus-buy and managed service versus self-hosted.
- Analyze total cost of ownership, operational burden and vendor lock-in.
- Understand platform engineering, internal developer platforms and standardization.
- Determine when distributed systems, microservices, event-driven architecture or complex technologies should not be used.
- Challenge the premise of the problem and request additional information before making decisions.
- Assess the long-term impact of technical debt, architecture evolution and organizational structure.
- Establish standards for reliability, security, observability, compatibility and maintainability.

### Evaluation Focus

- Nature.
- Limitations.
- Architecture.
- Decision framework.
- Multi-dimensional trade-offs.
- Reliability.
- Security.
- Data consistency.
- Failure isolation.
- Governance.
- Cost.
- Long-term implications.
- Systems thinking.
- Ability to challenge assumptions.
