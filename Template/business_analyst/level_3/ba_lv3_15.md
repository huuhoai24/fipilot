# Bộ Câu Hỏi Phỏng Vấn Business Analyst (Level 3) - Stakeholder Engagement & Soft Skills (15)

* **Role:** Business Analyst
* **Level:** Level 3
* **Experience:** 4 - 5 năm kinh nghiệm

---

## CÂU HỎI DỄ (3 câu - Trọng số: 0.1 / câu)

### Câu 1
* **Độ khó:** Dễ
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn quản lý expectations của stakeholders khi dự án bị delay. Communication strategy là gì?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Early và transparent communication
    keypoint_weight: 0.4
    description: Thông báo sớm nhất có thể, giải thích nguyên nhân rõ ràng, không đợi đến deadline mới báo.
  - id: KP2
    content: Impact analysis và options
    keypoint_weight: 0.35
    description: Trình bày impact cụ thể, đề xuất options: reduce scope, extend timeline, add resources.
  - id: KP3
    content: Revised plan và commitment
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Cung cấp revised timeline realistic, regular status updates, rebuild trust qua delivery.

### Câu 2
* **Độ khó:** Dễ
* **Câu hỏi:** Kỹ năng active listening quan trọng thế nào cho BA? Cho ví dụ technique bạn sử dụng.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Paraphrasing và summarizing
    keypoint_weight: 0.4
    description: Tóm tắt lại ý của stakeholder để confirm understanding, phát hiện miscommunication sớm.
  - id: KP2
    content: Probing questions
    keypoint_weight: 0.35
    description: Đặt câu hỏi follow-up để đi sâu hơn: 'Bạn có thể cho ví dụ?', 'Tại sao điều đó quan trọng?'
  - id: KP3
    content: Non-verbal awareness
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Chú ý body language, tone of voice, hesitation - indicators of unspoken concerns.

### Câu 3
* **Độ khó:** Dễ
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn onboard vào dự án đang chạy giữa chừng. Approach để catch up nhanh và contribute effectively?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Documentation review
    keypoint_weight: 0.35
    description: Review existing BRD, sprint backlogs, architecture docs, meeting notes, decision logs.
  - id: KP2
    content: Key stakeholder introductions
    keypoint_weight: 0.35
    description: 1-on-1 meetings với PM, tech lead, key business stakeholders để understand context và relationships.
  - id: KP3
    content: Quick wins identification
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Identify và deliver small valuable contributions early để build credibility và team trust.

## CÂU HỎI TRUNG BÌNH (4 câu - Trọng số: 0.1 / câu)

### Câu 4
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn negotiate scope với Product Owner muốn 'everything in first release'. Mô tả negotiation framework và techniques.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Data-driven negotiation
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Team velocity, historical delivery data, risk of overcommitment. Facts over opinions.
  - id: KP2
    content: MVP framing
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Reframe conversation: 'What's the minimum to validate?', 'What can we learn from Phase 1?'
  - id: KP3
    content: Trade-off visualization
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Scope-time-quality triangle, feature matrix with effort/value, visual priority exercise.
  - id: KP4
    content: Agreement documentation
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Document agreed scope, out-of-scope items with rationale, phasing plan for deferred items.

### Câu 5
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn mentor junior BAs. Mô tả mentoring approach và skills development framework.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Skill assessment và development plan
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Evaluate current skills vs IIBA BABOK competencies, create individualized development plan.
  - id: KP2
    content: Progressive responsibility delegation
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Start with documentation tasks, graduate to facilitation, then complex analysis. Guided autonomy.
  - id: KP3
    content: Constructive feedback cadence
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Regular 1-on-1s, document review feedback, post-meeting debriefs, 360 feedback.
  - id: KP4
    content: Knowledge transfer methods
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Pair analysis sessions, template library, case study discussions, community of practice.

### Câu 6
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn phải present bad news: dự án feasibility study cho thấy dự án không khả thi. Cách bạn communicate kết quả này?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Structured presentation
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Lead with objective findings, data-driven conclusions, avoid personal opinions.
  - id: KP2
    content: Alternative recommendations
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Không chỉ nói 'không khả thi'. Đề xuất alternatives: scaled-down version, different approach, phased exploration.
  - id: KP3
    content: Anticipate reactions
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Prepare responses cho likely pushback, have supporting data ready, acknowledge stakeholder investment.
  - id: KP4
    content: Next steps proposal
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Clear recommendation: kill, pivot, or further investigation. Decision criteria, timeline cho decision.

### Câu 7
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn lead requirements workshops cho cross-cultural team (US, Vietnam, Japan). Cultural considerations?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Communication style differences
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Direct vs indirect communication, high vs low context cultures. Adjust facilitation accordingly.
  - id: KP2
    content: Decision-making norms
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Consensus-based (Japan) vs individual authority (US). Allow pre-meeting discussions.
  - id: KP3
    content: Participation equity
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Some cultures hesitant to disagree publicly. Use anonymous input, small group discussions, written feedback.
  - id: KP4
    content: Language và timezone logistics
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Clear spoken English, written summaries, visual aids, rotating meeting times, async follow-ups.

## CÂU HỎI KHÓ (3 câu - Trọng số: 0.1 / câu)

### Câu 8
* **Độ khó:** Khó
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn đối mặt với political dynamics: 2 senior VPs disagree về direction dự án, mỗi bên muốn BA support position của mình. Approach?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Maintain objectivity
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: BA role: neutral facilitator. Present facts, data, và analysis without taking sides. Build trust with both parties.
  - id: KP2
    content: Structured decision framework
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Create objective evaluation criteria trước, get agreement on criteria, then evaluate options against criteria.
  - id: KP3
    content: Escalation strategy
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: If impasse continues, propose escalation to steering committee with clear options và recommendation.
  - id: KP4
    content: Documenting decisions và rationale
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Maintain detailed decision log, ensure transparency, protect BA from being scapegoated.

### Câu 9
* **Độ khó:** Khó
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn cần influence organizational adoption of BA best practices mà không có formal authority. Mô tả influence strategy.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Demonstrate value through results
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Pilot best practices trong 1-2 projects, measure improvement, create success stories.
  - id: KP2
    content: Build coalition of support
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Identify allies: progressive PMs, supportive sponsors, quality-minded developers. Grow grassroots movement.
  - id: KP3
    content: Education và awareness
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Brown bag sessions, blog posts, case studies, templates sharing, mentoring relationships.
  - id: KP4
    content: Executive sponsorship cultivation
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Connect BA practices improvement with business outcomes executives care about: quality, speed, cost.

### Câu 10
* **Độ khó:** Khó
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn facilitate strategic planning session cho C-suite team, translating business strategy thành technology roadmap. Approach?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Strategy decomposition
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Break strategic goals → strategic initiatives → capabilities needed → technology enablers. Traceability chain.
  - id: KP2
    content: Capability gap analysis
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Current vs required business capabilities, technology gaps, people/process gaps. Prioritization.
  - id: KP3
    content: Investment portfolio framing
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Categorize technology investments: Run (maintain), Grow (enhance), Transform (innovate). Budget allocation.
  - id: KP4
    content: Executive communication artifacts
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Strategic roadmap on single page, initiative cards with ROI, risk heat map, dependency timeline.
