# Bộ Câu Hỏi Phỏng Vấn Business Analyst (Level 3) - Domain Knowledge & Industry Analysis (10)

* **Role:** Business Analyst
* **Level:** Level 3
* **Experience:** 4 - 5 năm kinh nghiệm

---

## CÂU HỎI DỄ (3 câu - Trọng số: 0.1 / câu)

### Câu 1
* **Độ khó:** Dễ
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn tham gia dự án trong domain mới (banking/healthcare/retail). Approach để ramp-up domain knowledge nhanh nhất?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Structured learning approach
    keypoint_weight: 0.35
    description: Industry reports, regulatory documentation, competitor analysis, domain glossary creation.
  - id: KP2
    content: SME interviews và shadowing
    keypoint_weight: 0.35
    description: Phỏng vấn subject matter experts, observe current operations, attend business meetings.
  - id: KP3
    content: Process documentation review
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Review existing SOPs, system documentation, training materials, past project artifacts.

### Câu 2
* **Độ khó:** Dễ
* **Câu hỏi:** SWOT Analysis trong vai trò BA: bạn thực hiện nó khi nào và đầu ra được sử dụng thế nào?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats
    keypoint_weight: 0.4
    description: Framework đánh giá nội bộ (S, W) và ngoại vi (O, T) cho một tổ chức, product, hoặc process.
  - id: KP2
    content: Khi nào BA dùng SWOT
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Pre-project assessment, vendor evaluation, feasibility study, strategic planning input.
  - id: KP3
    content: Chuyển SWOT thành action items
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: SO strategies: leverage strengths for opportunities. WT strategies: mitigate weaknesses against threats.

### Câu 3
* **Độ khó:** Dễ
* **Câu hỏi:** Porter's Five Forces analysis là gì? BA áp dụng framework này cho market analysis như thế nào?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: 5 forces framework
    keypoint_weight: 0.4
    description: Competitive rivalry, supplier power, buyer power, threat of substitution, threat of new entry.
  - id: KP2
    content: Application trong product strategy
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Inform product positioning, pricing strategy, feature prioritization dựa trên competitive landscape.
  - id: KP3
    content: Data sources và analysis method
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Market reports, competitor products, customer interviews, industry benchmarks. Quantitative scoring khi có thể.

## CÂU HỎI TRUNG BÌNH (4 câu - Trọng số: 0.1 / câu)

### Câu 4
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn phân tích yêu cầu cho hệ thống trong ngành regulated (banking/pharma). Regulatory requirements ảnh hưởng thế nào đến BA approach?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Regulatory landscape mapping
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Identify applicable regulations, compliance requirements, reporting obligations, audit standards.
  - id: KP2
    content: Requirements categorization
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Phân biệt mandatory (regulatory) vs desired (business) requirements. Regulatory requirements non-negotiable.
  - id: KP3
    content: Compliance traceability
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Map mỗi regulatory clause → system requirement → test case → evidence of compliance.
  - id: KP4
    content: Change impact on compliance
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Assess compliance impact cho mọi change request, maintain regulatory change log.

### Câu 5
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn thực hiện competitive analysis cho product team. Mô tả framework và deliverables.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Competitor identification và categorization
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Direct, indirect, potential competitors. Market positioning map.
  - id: KP2
    content: Feature comparison matrix
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Structured comparison: features, pricing, UX, integrations, support. Scored evaluation.
  - id: KP3
    content: Gap và opportunity identification
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Identify unmet market needs, competitor weaknesses, potential differentiation areas.
  - id: KP4
    content: Ongoing monitoring plan
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Alerts for competitor changes, quarterly review cadence, product team integration.

### Câu 6
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn viết feasibility study cho dự án mới. Mô tả các dimensions và criteria đánh giá.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Technical feasibility
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Technology maturity, team capabilities, integration complexity, infrastructure requirements.
  - id: KP2
    content: Economic feasibility
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Cost-benefit analysis, ROI, payback period, funding availability.
  - id: KP3
    content: Operational feasibility
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Organizational readiness, user adoption likelihood, process change magnitude.
  - id: KP4
    content: Schedule feasibility
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Timeline constraints, resource availability, dependency on external factors, market window.

### Câu 7
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn tạo Business Model Canvas cho một digital product mới. Mô tả các building blocks và cách BA contribute.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: 9 building blocks
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Resources, Key Activities, Key Partnerships, Cost Structure.
  - id: KP2
    content: BA contribution areas
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: BA deep-dive vào customer segments (persona), value proposition (jobs-to-be-done), channels (user journey).
  - id: KP3
    content: Validation approach
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Lean Startup: hypothesis → experiment → learn. MVP definition, success metrics cho mỗi hypothesis.
  - id: KP4
    content: Iteration và pivot signals
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Metrics indicating pivot needed: low adoption, negative unit economics, market shift. Decision framework.

## CÂU HỎI KHÓ (3 câu - Trọng số: 0.1 / câu)

### Câu 8
* **Độ khó:** Khó
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn cần phân tích và document business requirements cho nền tảng Open Banking API tuân thủ PSD2. Trình bày approach end-to-end.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: PSD2 regulatory requirements mapping
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Strong Customer Authentication (SCA), consent management, Account Information Service (AIS), Payment Initiation Service (PIS).
  - id: KP2
    content: API design requirements
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: RESTful API specs, OAuth 2.0 flows, data models cho account/transaction/payment endpoints, error handling.
  - id: KP3
    content: Third-party provider management
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: TPP onboarding process, certificate management, rate limiting, sandbox environment requirements.
  - id: KP4
    content: Security và monitoring requirements
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Transaction risk analysis, fraud detection, real-time monitoring, incident response procedures.

### Câu 9
* **Độ khó:** Khó
* **Câu hỏi:** Thiết kế một Market Entry Analysis framework cho công ty muốn expand sang 3 thị trường Đông Nam Á mới. BA deliverables là gì?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Market attractiveness assessment
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Market size (TAM/SAM/SOM), growth rate, competitive intensity, regulatory environment, cultural factors.
  - id: KP2
    content: Entry mode analysis
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Direct investment, partnership, acquisition, licensing. Decision matrix với criteria: risk, control, speed, cost.
  - id: KP3
    content: Localization requirements
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Language, payment methods, legal/regulatory compliance, cultural UX adaptations, local partnerships.
  - id: KP4
    content: Go-to-market strategy inputs
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Customer segmentation per market, pricing strategy, channel strategy, success metrics per quarter.

### Câu 10
* **Độ khó:** Khó
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn phân tích impact của emerging technology (AI/Blockchain/IoT) lên business model hiện tại. Trình bày structured approach.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Technology maturity assessment
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Gartner Hype Cycle positioning, current adoption in industry, technology readiness level.
  - id: KP2
    content: Business model impact analysis
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Impact trên mỗi BMC building block: new value propositions, disrupted channels, changed cost structure.
  - id: KP3
    content: Opportunity và threat mapping
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Identify use cases: cost reduction, new revenue streams, competitive advantages, và disruption threats.
  - id: KP4
    content: Innovation roadmap
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Phased approach: experiment (POC) → pilot → scale. Investment thesis, success criteria, go/no-go gates.
