# Bộ Câu Hỏi Phỏng Vấn Business Analyst (Level 3) - Project Management Skills cho BA (18)

* **Role:** Business Analyst
* **Level:** Level 3
* **Experience:** 4 - 5 năm kinh nghiệm

---

## CÂU HỎI DỄ (3 câu - Trọng số: 0.1 / câu)

### Câu 1
* **Độ khó:** Dễ
* **Câu hỏi:** BA và PM roles overlap ở đâu? Bạn phân biệt và collaborate với PM thế nào?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Overlap areas
    keypoint_weight: 0.35
    description: Scope management, stakeholder communication, risk identification, change management.
  - id: KP2
    content: Clear boundaries
    keypoint_weight: 0.35
    description: PM: timeline, budget, resources, delivery. BA: requirements, analysis, business value, solution design.
  - id: KP3
    content: Collaboration model
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Joint stakeholder meetings, BA inputs cho project planning, PM supports BA access to stakeholders.

### Câu 2
* **Độ khó:** Dễ
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn sử dụng Jira/Azure DevOps để quản lý requirements. Best practices cho BA?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Requirements hierarchy
    keypoint_weight: 0.4
    description: Epic → Feature → User Story → Task. Clear descriptions, acceptance criteria, và links giữa levels.
  - id: KP2
    content: Workflow customization
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Status workflow phản ánh BA process: Draft → Review → Approved → In Development → Done.
  - id: KP3
    content: Reporting và tracking
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Burndown charts, velocity tracking, requirements coverage reports, traceability views.

### Câu 3
* **Độ khó:** Dễ
* **Câu hỏi:** Risk identification từ góc nhìn BA. Bạn identify và communicate project risks như thế nào?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: BA-specific risk categories
    keypoint_weight: 0.4
    description: Requirements risks: scope creep, ambiguity, missing requirements, stakeholder unavailability, technology uncertainty.
  - id: KP2
    content: Risk assessment
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Probability × Impact matrix, qualitative assessment, risk triggers identification.
  - id: KP3
    content: Mitigation strategies
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Early prototyping, incremental delivery, spike stories, fallback requirements, contingency plans.

---

## CÂU HỎI TRUNG BÌNH (4 câu - Trọng số: 0.1 / câu)

### Câu 4
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn manage BA workload across 3 concurrent projects. Mô tả prioritization và time management approach.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Priority assessment framework
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Urgency-importance matrix, project deadlines, sprint commitments, stakeholder expectations.
  - id: KP2
    content: Capacity planning
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Allocate percentage time per project, block calendar for deep work, buffer for ad-hoc requests.
  - id: KP3
    content: Communication management
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Set expectations with each project team, weekly status updates, proactive delay communication.
  - id: KP4
    content: Delegation và efficiency
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Leverage templates, reuse artifacts, delegate documentation to junior BAs, automate repetitive tasks.

### Câu 5
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn estimate BA effort cho một new project. Approach và factors cần consider?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Estimation technique selection
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Expert judgment, analogous estimation, parametric (stories per sprint), three-point estimation (PERT).
  - id: KP2
    content: Effort drivers cho BA work
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Number of stakeholder groups, domain complexity, integration count, regulatory requirements, team experience.
  - id: KP3
    content: Activity breakdown
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Elicitation (30%), analysis (25%), documentation (20%), validation (15%), support (10%). Adjust per project.
  - id: KP4
    content: Contingency và assumptions
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Document assumptions, add 15-30% contingency, review và re-estimate at phase gates.

### Câu 6
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn track requirements throughout project lifecycle. Mô tả requirements lifecycle management process.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Requirements states
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Proposed → Analyzed → Approved → Designed → Implemented → Validated → Deployed → Retired.
  - id: KP2
    content: Baseline management
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Requirements baseline at key milestones, formal change process post-baseline, version control.
  - id: KP3
    content: Traceability maintenance
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Bi-directional traceability: requirement ↔ design ↔ test case ↔ defect. Impact analysis capability.
  - id: KP4
    content: Requirements metrics
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Volatility index, completeness ratio, approval cycle time, defects traced to requirements.

### Câu 7
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn participate trong project retrospective. Từ góc nhìn BA, bạn focus vào improvement areas nào?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Requirements quality review
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Defects from ambiguous requirements, scope changes frequency, missed requirements discovered late.
  - id: KP2
    content: Elicitation effectiveness
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Stakeholder engagement level, number of clarification rounds, requirement churn rate.
  - id: KP3
    content: Process improvement opportunities
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Templates that need updating, tools that need changing, collaboration patterns that worked/didn't.
  - id: KP4
    content: Knowledge management
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Lessons learned documentation, reusable artifacts, domain knowledge capture, team knowledge sharing.

## CÂU HỎI KHÓ (3 câu - Trọng số: 0.1 / câu)

### Câu 8
* **Độ khó:** Khó
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn establish BA governance framework cho program với 5 workstreams và 8 BAs. Mô tả operating model.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: BA operating model
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Centralized BA lead, workstream BA assignments, shared standards, regular BA sync meetings.
  - id: KP2
    content: Cross-workstream requirements management
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Shared requirements repository, dependency tracking, common glossary, reusable components.
  - id: KP3
    content: Quality assurance process
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Peer review rotation, requirements quality metrics, calibration sessions, retrospective actions.
  - id: KP4
    content: Reporting và escalation
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: BA status dashboard, risk/issue escalation path, resource allocation conflicts resolution.

### Câu 9
* **Độ khó:** Khó
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn phải justify BA team investment cho executive board showing ROI of BA activities. Approach?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Cost of poor requirements quantification
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Industry data: fixing requirements defects in production costs 50-200x more than in requirements phase.
  - id: KP2
    content: BA contribution metrics
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Requirements defect rate reduction, scope change reduction, rework elimination, project success rate improvement.
  - id: KP3
    content: Business outcome attribution
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Link BA activities to business outcomes: faster time-to-market, higher user adoption, fewer post-launch issues.
  - id: KP4
    content: Benchmarking
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Compare with vs without BA engagement, industry benchmarks, before/after case studies.

### Câu 10
* **Độ khó:** Khó
* **Câu hỏi:** Thiết kế một BA Maturity Assessment framework cho organization. Dimensions, levels, và improvement roadmap.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Maturity dimensions
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Process maturity, people capability, tools và technology, organizational alignment, value delivery.
  - id: KP2
    content: Maturity levels
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Level 1: Initial (ad-hoc). Level 2: Managed (basic standards). Level 3: Defined (consistent). Level 4: Quantitatively Managed. Level 5: Optimizing.
  - id: KP3
    content: Assessment methodology
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Self-assessment questionnaire, interviews, artifact review, benchmarking against IIBA standards.
  - id: KP4
    content: Improvement roadmap
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Priority areas based on current level, quick wins, capability building plan, investment requirements.
