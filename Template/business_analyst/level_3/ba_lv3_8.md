# Bộ Câu Hỏi Phỏng Vấn Business Analyst (Level 3) - Change Management & Communication (8)

* **Role:** Business Analyst
* **Level:** Level 3
* **Experience:** 4 - 5 năm kinh nghiệm

---

## CÂU HỎI DỄ (3 câu - Trọng số: 0.1 / câu)

### Câu 1
* **Độ khó:** Dễ
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn áp dụng ADKAR model trong change management như thế nào? Cho ví dụ dự án thực tế.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement
    keypoint_weight: 0.4
    description: 5 phases: nhận thức về sự thay đổi, mong muốn tham gia, kiến thức cần thiết, khả năng thực hiện, duy trì thay đổi.
  - id: KP2
    content: BA role trong mỗi phase
    keypoint_weight: 0.35
    description: BA hỗ trợ communication plan, training materials, user guides, post-implementation support.
  - id: KP3
    content: Đo lường adoption
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Survey, system usage metrics, support ticket trends để đánh giá hiệu quả change management.

### Câu 2
* **Độ khó:** Dễ
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn tạo Communication Plan cho dự án có nhiều stakeholder groups khác nhau. Các thành phần chính là gì?
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Stakeholder analysis đầu vào
    keypoint_weight: 0.35
    description: Xác định stakeholder groups, communication needs, preferred channels, frequency.
  - id: KP2
    content: Message tailoring
    keypoint_weight: 0.35
    description: Customize nội dung cho từng audience: C-level (impact, ROI), end-users (what changes), IT (technical details).
  - id: KP3
    content: Feedback mechanism
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Two-way communication: feedback surveys, Q&A sessions, office hours, dedicated channels.

### Câu 3
* **Độ khó:** Dễ
* **Câu hỏi:** Làm thế nào để bạn present complex analysis findings cho non-technical stakeholders? Cho ví dụ kỹ thuật cụ thể.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Storytelling với data
    keypoint_weight: 0.4
    description: Cấu trúc: problem statement → data evidence → insight → recommendation. Dùng analogies cho technical concepts.
  - id: KP2
    content: Visualization phù hợp audience
    keypoint_weight: 0.35
    description: Executive summary dashboards, infographics thay vì tables phức tạp, progressive detail.
  - id: KP3
    content: Actionable recommendations
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Luôn kết thúc bằng clear next steps, decision points, và timeline.

## CÂU HỎI TRUNG BÌNH (4 câu - Trọng số: 0.1 / câu)

### Câu 4
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn quản lý change requests trong dự án large-scale ERP implementation. Mô tả Change Control Board (CCB) process.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Change request lifecycle
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Submit → impact analysis → CCB review → approve/reject/defer → implement → verify.
  - id: KP2
    content: Impact analysis template
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Scope, schedule, cost, risk, dependencies, regression impact assessment cho mỗi change request.
  - id: KP3
    content: CCB composition và decision criteria
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Members: PM, BA lead, Tech lead, Business sponsor. Criteria: business value, urgency, effort, risk.
  - id: KP4
    content: Change request metrics
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Track volume, approval rate, implementation success, scope creep indicators.

### Câu 5
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn cần train 200+ end-users cho hệ thống mới. Thiết kế training strategy từ planning đến evaluation.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Training needs assessment
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Phân tích current skills, target skills, gap cho mỗi user group, learning style preferences.
  - id: KP2
    content: Multi-modal training delivery
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Instructor-led training, e-learning modules, hands-on workshops, quick reference guides, video tutorials.
  - id: KP3
    content: Training environment
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Sandbox environment với realistic data, pre-configured scenarios, parallel run với old system.
  - id: KP4
    content: Effectiveness measurement
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Kirkpatrick model: Reaction, Learning, Behavior, Results. Pre/post assessment scores.

### Câu 6
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Mô tả cách bạn xử lý resistance to change từ key users khi triển khai hệ thống mới thay thế quy trình thủ công.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Root cause analysis cho resistance
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Hiểu lý do: fear of job loss, comfort zone, lack of trust, previous bad experiences, workload concerns.
  - id: KP2
    content: Involvement strategy
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Đưa resistors vào pilot group, cho participate trong design decisions, champion network.
  - id: KP3
    content: Quick wins demonstration
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Show immediate benefits: time savings, error reduction, auto-reporting thay vì manual.
  - id: KP4
    content: Executive sponsorship leverage
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Engage senior leadership để reinforce change message, remove blockers, allocate resources.

### Câu 7
* **Độ khó:** Trung bình
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn viết Business Requirements Document (BRD) cho dự án cross-functional. Mô tả structure và review process.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: BRD structure chuẩn
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Executive summary, business objectives, scope, stakeholders, requirements, assumptions, constraints, appendices.
  - id: KP2
    content: Review và approval workflow
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Peer review → stakeholder review → formal walkthrough → sign-off. Track comments và resolution.
  - id: KP3
    content: Version control
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Document versioning, change log, baseline management, distributed review tracking.
  - id: KP4
    content: Requirements traceability setup
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Unique ID cho mỗi requirement, link với test cases, design artifacts, và acceptance criteria.

## CÂU HỎI KHÓ (3 câu - Trọng số: 0.1 / câu)

### Câu 8
* **Độ khó:** Khó
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn lead BA activities cho một M&A (Merger & Acquisition) technology integration. Mô tả approach và key deliverables.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Due diligence analysis
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Đánh giá technology stack, data assets, system landscape, technical debt, license/contract obligations.
  - id: KP2
    content: System consolidation roadmap
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Identify overlapping systems, sunset plan, data migration strategy, integration architecture.
  - id: KP3
    content: Process harmonization
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Map duplicate business processes, design unified TO-BE processes, change management cho both organizations.
  - id: KP4
    content: Day 1 readiness checklist
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Critical integrations for day 1: email, directory, financial reporting, customer-facing systems.

### Câu 9
* **Độ khó:** Khó
* **Câu hỏi:** Thiết kế một BA Center of Excellence (CoE) cho tổ chức enterprise. Mô tả structure, processes, và metrics.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: CoE organizational structure
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Centralized vs federated model, BA roles hierarchy, reporting lines, CoE lead responsibilities.
  - id: KP2
    content: BA methodology và standards
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Unified BA methodology, templates library, tool standardization, quality gates.
  - id: KP3
    content: Capability development program
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Skill matrix, career path, training curriculum, mentoring program, certification support.
  - id: KP4
    content: CoE value metrics
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Project success rate, requirements defect rate, stakeholder satisfaction, BA utilization, knowledge reuse.

### Câu 10
* **Độ khó:** Khó
* **Câu hỏi:** Bạn cần tạo một comprehensive Organizational Impact Assessment cho dự án automation thay thế 30% manual tasks. Trình bày framework.
* **expected_key_points:**
  - id: KP1
    content: Job role impact mapping
    keypoint_weight: 0.3
    description: Phân tích từng role: tasks eliminated, tasks modified, new tasks created, reskilling needs.
  - id: KP2
    content: Workforce transition plan
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Redeployment options, reskilling programs, voluntary separation packages, timeline cho transitions.
  - id: KP3
    content: Process and system interdependencies
    keypoint_weight: 0.25
    description: Map cascading impacts: upstream/downstream processes, reporting changes, compliance implications.
  - id: KP4
    content: Communication và stakeholder management
    keypoint_weight: 0.2
    description: Sensitive communication plan, union/works council engagement, transparency vs anxiety management.
