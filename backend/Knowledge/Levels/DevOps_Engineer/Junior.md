# DevOps Engineer - Junior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the operating mechanisms of a CI/CD pipeline.
- Explain the flow of build, test, artifact and deployment.
- Understand container lifecycle and Kubernetes architecture at a basic level.
- Understand Pods, Deployments, Services, ConfigMaps and Secrets.
- Compare Virtual Machine and Container.
- Understand Infrastructure as Code and configuration management at a conceptual level.
- Understand basic networking in cloud and container environments.
- Explain load balancer, reverse proxy and DNS.
- Understand monitoring, metrics, logs and tracing at a mechanistic level.
- Understand auto scaling, health check and rolling update.
- Understand authentication, authorization and secret management.
- Identify common failure cases in deployment and infrastructure.
- Explain basic trade-offs among automation, reliability and operational complexity.

### Evaluation Focus

- Mechanisms.
- Causes.
- Basic assumptions.
- Method comparison.
- Basic trade-offs.
- CI/CD.
- Cloud.
- Container.
- Monitoring.
