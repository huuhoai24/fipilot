# Business Analyst - Middle Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Deeply understand how to identify a business problem before describing a solution.
- Analyze business objectives, capability gaps, root causes and expected outcomes.
- Distinguish among symptoms, root causes, business needs and solution requests.
- Analyze a stakeholder ecosystem with multiple conflicting interests and objectives.
- Evaluate requirement priority based on business value, risk, dependency, urgency and implementation cost.
- Deeply understand requirement decomposition, refinement, validation and verification.
- Analyze requirement conflicts, ambiguity, duplication, inconsistency and missing requirements.
- Understand impact analysis of changes on processes, data, systems, policies, operations and stakeholders.
- Evaluate trade-offs among business value, scope, cost, timeline, compliance and operational complexity.
- Analyze end-to-end business processes, handoffs, bottlenecks, exception paths and control points.
- Understand process optimization, waste elimination and control design at a theoretical level.
- Analyze business rules, decision logic and policy constraints.
- Understand conceptual data models, logical data relationships, data ownership and data quality requirements.
- Analyze integration requirements, interface dependencies and cross-system data flows.
- Understand non-functional requirements such as performance, security, availability, usability, auditability and scalability.
- Evaluate solution options based on fit-gap, benefit, risk and constraint.
- Understand business cases, cost-benefit analysis and feasibility analysis at an analytical level.
- Understand KPIs, outcome metrics, leading indicators and lagging indicators.
- Evaluate requirement coverage and traceability end-to-end from objective to test to outcome.
- Understand assumption validation, risk analysis and decision rationale.
- Analyze change readiness, stakeholder resistance and adoption risk at a conceptual level.
- Articulate how to validate business hypotheses using data, stakeholder evidence or process evidence.
- Avoid concluding on a solution without clarifying the problem, scope and success criteria.

### Evaluation Focus

- Problem framing.
- Root cause.
- Stakeholder conflict.
- Requirement decomposition.
- Impact analysis.
- Process optimization.
- Data and integration requirements.
- Non-functional requirements.
- Solution evaluation.
- Business value.
- Risk and assumptions.
- End-to-end traceability.
