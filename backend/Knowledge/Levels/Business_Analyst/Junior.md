# Business Analyst - Junior Level Knowledge Depth & Evaluation

### Knowledge Depth

The candidate should:

- Understand the mechanism of converting business needs into clear and verifiable requirements.
- Explain the relationship among business objectives, stakeholder needs, requirements and solution scope.
- Understand assumptions, constraints, dependencies and risks at a basic level.
- Analyze stakeholders by interest, influence, impact and communication need.
- Understand when to use interview, workshop, observation, prototyping or document analysis.
- Distinguish between valid, clear, complete, consistent, feasible and testable requirements.
- Understand how to write user stories, use cases, business rules and acceptance criteria.
- Explain the differences between functional requirements and non-functional requirements.
- Understand process modeling at a mechanistic level, including as-is, to-be, actor, trigger, activity, decision and exception flow.
- Understand data requirements, business data definitions and basic data mapping.
- Compare common prioritization techniques such as MoSCoW, ranking and value-versus-effort.
- Understand requirement traceability and the impact of requirement changes.
- Understand backlog, refinement, Definition of Ready and Definition of Done at a conceptual level.
- Understand the role of a BA in Agile, Waterfall and hybrid delivery models.
- Identify scope creep, conflicting requirements and hidden stakeholder needs.
- Understand the relationship among requirements, test scenarios and acceptance.
- Explain basic trade-offs among scope, time, cost and quality.
- Understand root cause analysis at a basic level through Five Whys, fishbone or problem statement.

### Evaluation Focus

- Requirement formation mechanism.
- Stakeholder analysis.
- Requirement quality.
- Process and data modeling.
- Prioritization.
- Traceability.
- Basic change impact.
- Basic trade-offs.
- Basic root cause.
